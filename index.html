Our Story

Born in Toronto.
Built on Flavour.

Poutine Hub started with a simple idea: take Canada's most beloved comfort food and give it a bold, multicultural twist that reflects the true spirit of Toronto.

We opened our first location on Parliament Street in the heart of Regent Park. The response was overwhelming — Toronto was hungry for something new.

Our fusion poutines — from the legendary Butter Chicken Poutine to the fiery Tikka Masala Poutine — quickly became the talk of the city. We expanded to Dundas Street East to serve even more of the city we love.

🔒 Privacy & Security

🔐 Secured with 256-bit TLS Encryption

What We Collect

  • Name, email, phone — used only to confirm your order and send pickup/delivery alerts
  • Delivery address — used only for the current order, stored locally on your device
  • Order history — stored on your device only, never uploaded to a third-party server

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We never store credit or debit card numbers. All card payments are handled directly by Stripe — a PCI DSS Level 1 certified processor. Your card data never touches our systems.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing
  • We do not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers

How Your Data Is Protected

🔐 Connection EncryptionTLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
💳 Card PaymentsStripe PCI DSS L1
🔑 AuthenticationFirebase Auth (Google)
📦 Order DataDevice-only (localStorage)
📧 Email ConfirmationEncrypted transit

Your Rights (PIPEDA — Canada)

  • You may request deletion of any personal data we hold by emailing us at privacy@poutinehub.ca
  • You may opt out of order confirmation emails by contacting us directly
  • Data is retained only as long as needed to fulfill your order
